# Petalspire background music: licenses

In the game (v0.7.2), `audio/music/` holds the four tracks below, renamed:
- town-theme.mp3 = town-theme_harp-recorder.mp3
- a-small-fire-will-do.mp3 = a-small-fire-will-do_guitar.mp3
- meadow-thoughts.mp3 = meadow-thoughts_harp.mp3
- a-new-town.mp3 = a-new-town_harp.mp3

The spare (calm-fireplace) is not shipped.

Also in the playlist: morning-on-the-hill.mp3 and morning-on-the-hill-2.mp3 ("Morning on the Hill", takes 1 and 2), Petalspire's own instrumentals. They are the game owner's songs, not CC0 tracks, and need no credit.
