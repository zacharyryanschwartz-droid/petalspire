Privacy policyPetalspire · Schwarze Spiele

Petalspire is made by Schwarze Spiele (Zachary Schwartz). This page describes what the game and the iPhone app actually do with information. It was last updated on 9 November 2026.

Questions: schwarzespiele@mail.grokbot.com

On your device

Your garden is saved on the device you play on. That save includes things like coins, jewels, plants, settings, and whether you own No More Ads or a pack. In a browser it lives in local storage. The iPhone app keeps the same save and also copies it into the app’s preferences so it can be restored after an update. We do not upload that garden save to our servers.

Anonymous play and invites

If the device can reach our game service (Appwrite, hosted at nyc.cloud.appwrite.io), the game creates an anonymous account. There is no name, email, or password. We store that anonymous id, the game name, whether you are on the web or the iPhone app, the app version, when you first played, when you were last seen, and a session count. This is so we can tell how the game is being played. It is not used to advertise to you.

If you use an invite code, we also store the code, the anonymous ids of the two gardens that are linked, a garden level number, and whether the friend reached level 3. We do not read your contacts, and we do not receive your social-media account if you tap a follow link. Those links just open the page in the browser.

Advertising

The iPhone app shows ads with Google AdMob. After you finish the opening guide, and not the first time you open the app, we may ask permission to track you across apps (Apple’s App Tracking prompt). The advertising identifier is used only if you allow that. If you decline, ads can still appear without that identifier.

In regions where Google requires a consent choice, the app shows Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) before requesting ads. Google’s own policy is at policies.google.com/privacy. How AdMob uses data is described at support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

The website version of the game does not use AdMob. Its banner promotes our own book and our upcoming game, Market Plot.

Payments

Purchases in the iPhone app are handled by Apple. We receive the product you bought and a transaction id so we can add jewels, turn off ads, or unlock a pack. We never receive your card number. The website does not sell anything.

What we don’t do

We do not sell your information. We do not ask for your real name, email, or precise location. The garden save stays on your device.

Children

Petalspire is not directed at children under 13. The AdMob setup is a general-audience app: it is not tagged as child-directed, and it is not tagged for users under the age of consent.
